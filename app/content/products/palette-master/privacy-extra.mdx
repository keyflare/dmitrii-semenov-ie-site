## Palette Master Notes

Palette Master is an offline single-player color puzzle game. The app does not use accounts, does
not ask players to create a profile, and does not provide a server-side user data deletion flow.

The Android release contains advertising. Google AdMob may process app activity, app information,
performance information, device identifiers, or similar technical signals according to Google's
advertising and data safety policies.

If you contact support by email, the information you choose to include in that email is processed so
the support request can be read and answered.
