# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how **Palette Master** ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and
shares information when you use the App.

The App is developed and operated by **Dmitrii Semenov IE**. If you have questions, contact us at
**support@keyflare.studio**.

## Information We Collect

Palette Master does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, phone number,
contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

We may process the following information:

### Gameplay and app usage data

We collect analytics events that help us understand how the App is used, such as:

- level started and completed;
- number of moves;
- hint usage;
- settings changes, such as sound, vibration, or theme;
- ad loading, showing, clicking, failure, and reward events;
- opening screens such as options, levels, about, contact, or privacy policy.

### Device, app, diagnostics, and crash data

We use AppMetrica to collect app analytics, diagnostics, and crash reports. This may include device
and app information, technical identifiers, operating system information, app version, crash logs,
and error diagnostics.

### Advertising data

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads, including interstitial ads between levels and rewarded ads
for hints.

Google AdMob and related Google services may process information such as advertising identifiers,
device information, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, and
consent choices. On iOS, the App may request App Tracking Transparency permission to use the device
advertising identifier for more relevant ads and ad measurement.

### Consent and privacy choices

The App uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show consent and privacy choice forms where
required or available. You can reopen available privacy choices from the App's Options screen.

### Local game data

The App stores game data on your device, such as:

- game progress;
- selected level;
- current game session;
- sound, vibration, and theme settings;
- onboarding state;
- experiment or debug-related settings.

On Android, this local app data may be included in Android backup or device transfer if enabled in
your device or Google account settings.

### Contact emails

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message so we can
respond.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide and improve gameplay;
- save your progress and settings;
- show ads and rewarded ads;
- manage advertising consent and privacy choices;
- measure app usage and ad performance;
- detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical issues;
- respond to support requests.

## Legal Bases

Where required by law, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

- your consent, especially for personalized ads and tracking where required;
- our legitimate interests in maintaining, securing, and improving the App;
- performance of the service, such as saving progress and app settings;
- compliance with legal obligations.

## Advertising Choices

You can manage advertising consent and privacy choices through the App's **Options -> Privacy
choices** entry when it is available.

You can also manage advertising identifiers and tracking permissions through your device settings:

- on iOS, through App Tracking Transparency and Apple privacy settings;
- on Android, through Google advertising ID and privacy settings.

If you decline personalized ads or tracking, the App may still show ads, but they may be
non-personalized or limited depending on your region, platform settings, and Google's ad serving
rules.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money.

We share or allow processing of information only as needed for:

- analytics and crash reporting through AppMetrica;
- advertising and consent management through Google AdMob and UMP;
- support communication when you contact us;
- legal compliance, security, or fraud prevention if required.

Some privacy laws may treat personalized advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising as
"sharing". You can manage these choices through the App's privacy choices and device settings where
available.

## Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the App, or uninstall the App. If
Android backup is enabled, backed-up data may remain according to your Google account and device
backup settings.

Analytics, diagnostics, crash, advertising, and consent data are retained by our third-party service
providers according to their own retention policies.

Support emails are retained as long as needed to respond to your request and maintain support
records.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has
provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Advertising and analytics SDKs may process technical data as described in this Policy. Parents and
guardians can manage ad personalization, tracking, and app permissions through device settings.

## International Data Transfers

The App and its third-party services may process information on servers located outside your country.
Where required, such transfers are handled according to applicable data protection laws and the
policies of the relevant service providers.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method
of transmission or storage is completely secure.
