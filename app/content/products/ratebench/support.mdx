## Development status

Ratebench is **In development** for Android and iOS. Features, calculations, data sources, and
subscription behavior may change before release. There are no public store links yet.

## Reporting a problem

When reporting a problem, please include:

- Android or iOS;
- device model and operating-system version;
- Ratebench app version or build, if visible;
- the affected feature;
- steps that reproduce the problem;
- what you expected to happen; and
- what happened instead.

Do not include passwords, private keys, seed phrases, payment card details, or other confidential
financial information.

## Rates are stale or unavailable

Check your internet connection and try again. Ratebench may show the latest cached rates when the
network or supporting service is unavailable. Cached rates can be outdated and should not be treated
as live or transaction-ready quotes.

If a problem continues, include the affected asset pair and approximate time in your support email.
You do not need to include a financial amount or private account information.

## Local history and notes

Calculation history, optional notes, selected assets, and preferences are generally stored on your
device. Clearing app data or uninstalling Ratebench may remove them. A copy may remain in an
operating-system backup or device transfer managed through your Apple, Google, or device settings.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are planned but are not yet publicly available.

When subscriptions launch:

- use **Restore purchases** in Ratebench to refresh eligible access after reinstalling or changing
  devices;
- manage or cancel an Apple subscription in
  [Apple subscription settings](https://support.apple.com/118428);
- manage or cancel a Google Play subscription in
  [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions);
- remember that uninstalling the App does not cancel a subscription.

For a billing problem, include the platform and transaction date in your email. Do not email your
full receipt, payment card number, bank details, or account password.
