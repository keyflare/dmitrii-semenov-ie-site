This Privacy Policy explains how **Ratebench** ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you
use the Ratebench mobile application for Android or iOS, its supporting rate service, and related
pages that link to this Policy.

Ratebench is developed and operated by **Dmitrii Semenov, Individual Entrepreneur, Armenia**. For
privacy questions, contact **support@keyflare.studio**.

Ratebench is a currency and crypto-asset reference, conversion, and calculation tool. It does not
provide user accounts, execute exchanges, hold funds or assets, or process payments itself.

## Information stored on your device

Ratebench stores information locally so its features work and your preferences remain available.
This may include:

- exchange calculation history, including dates, asset pairs, entered amounts, rates, fees,
  calculated results, and optional notes;
- selected currencies and crypto-assets, recently used assets, and saved rate pairs;
- appearance and other app settings; and
- cached market data, asset details, and currency icon references.

This information is not sent to us merely because it is stored on your device. It may be included in
backups or device-to-device transfers managed by Apple, Google, your device manufacturer, or your
operating system, depending on your settings.

You can remove local information by deleting history records, clearing the App's data, or
uninstalling the App. Copies may remain in backups controlled by you or your platform provider.

## Rate requests, search, and service logs

When Ratebench retrieves market information, it sends the supporting service the data needed to
answer the request. This may include fiat currency codes, crypto-asset identifiers, requested asset
pairs, and text entered into the crypto-asset search field.

Please do not enter personal or confidential information into the asset search field. Search text
may be forwarded by the supporting service to a market-data provider to return matching assets.

The supporting service and its hosting provider may automatically process technical request data,
such as IP address, request date and time, requested endpoint, response status, user-agent or
network information supplied with the request, and security or error logs.

## Analytics and diagnostics

Ratebench uses **AppMetrica**, an analytics and diagnostics service provided by Yandex, to understand
how the App is used and to identify technical problems. Depending on platform, SDK version,
configuration, and device settings, this information may include:

- app launches, sessions, screens opened, taps, and feature usage;
- selected asset identifiers or symbols, feature source, item indexes, result counts, and status
  flags;
- app version, operating system, device model or characteristics, language, network information,
  and app- or device-level identifiers;
- IP address and approximate location inferred from it; and
- crash reports, stack traces, error descriptions, performance information, and diagnostics.

Our custom analytics events are not designed to send the amounts or free-text notes you enter in
exchange calculations. Diagnostic reports may nevertheless contain information incidentally present
in an error context.

Ratebench does not contain third-party advertising. The Android version is configured without the
AppMetrica advertising-identifier module and removes the Android advertising-ID permission.
Ratebench does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. AppMetrica may still
process non-advertising app or device identifiers.

For more information, see
[AppMetrica data collection](https://appmetrica.yandex.com/docs/en/data-collection/),
[AppMetrica SDK data categories](https://appmetrica.yandex.com/docs/en/sdk/platforms), and the
[Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/).

## Subscriptions and purchase information

When subscriptions become available, purchases will be offered through Apple's App Store or Google
Play. Apple or Google processes the purchase and payment method under its own terms and privacy
notice.

Ratebench may receive or process purchase information needed to verify, provide, and restore paid
access. This may include product and transaction identifiers, an App Store receipt or Google Play
purchase token, subscription or entitlement status, renewal or expiration information, and
storefront or platform information.

Ratebench does not directly receive your full payment card number, bank-account credentials, or
other payment method details from Apple or Google.

## Communications you choose to send

If you contact us, including through **Contact Us** or **Report a Bug**, we receive the information
you voluntarily provide through your email service. This may include your email address, name,
message, attachments, and technical details you include.

Ratebench opens your chosen email application with our contact address and a suggested subject. It
does not automatically read or send your email.

## Information Ratebench does not request

Ratebench does not currently request:

- user accounts or profile information;
- payment card, bank-account, wallet, or transaction credentials;
- custody of money, fiat currency, or crypto-assets;
- contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings, health data, or precise GPS location; or
- information for third-party advertising.

If these practices change, this Policy will be updated and any notice or consent required by law
will be provided.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- provide rate, search, conversion, calculation, history, preference, and subscription features;
- retrieve, cache, and display market data;
- verify and restore subscription access when paid features become available;
- maintain, secure, troubleshoot, measure, and improve the Service;
- detect abuse, excessive automated traffic, fraud, or security incidents;
- respond to questions, feedback, and bug reports; and
- comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on performance of the service you
request, legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, consent where requested, or
legal obligations.

## Third-party services and disclosures

Information may be processed by these categories of recipients when reasonably necessary:

- **AppMetrica / Yandex** for app analytics, diagnostics, and crash reporting;
- **Render** for hosting the supporting service, delivering responses, security, monitoring, and
  technical logs;
- **CoinGecko, Frankfurter, and Open Exchange Rates when configured** for crypto-asset information
  and fiat rates. These providers generally receive requests from the supporting service rather
  than directly from your device;
- **Apple and Google** for app distribution, subscriptions, operating-system services, and backups
  or device transfers you enable;
- professional advisers, authorities, or a successor organization when permitted or required by
  law.

We do not sell personal information for money, and Ratebench does not use personal information for
cross-context behavioral advertising.

Third-party services process information under their own terms, privacy notices, settings, and
retention practices. Their services may change independently of Ratebench.

## Retention and security

Local information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, uninstall the
App, or it is removed by operating-system behavior. Supporting-service logs, analytics,
diagnostics, purchase records, and support communications are retained only as reasonably needed
for the purposes described above, including security, support, subscription verification, and legal
obligations. Third-party providers apply their own retention settings and policies.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect
information, including encrypted network transport, access controls, and data minimization. No
method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and subject to legal exceptions, you may have rights to request access,
correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent.

Ratebench has no user-account portal. To make a request, contact
[support@keyflare.studio](mailto:support@keyflare.studio?subject=Ratebench%20privacy%20request).
Because some analytics data is pseudonymous and Ratebench does not maintain user accounts, we may
not be able to associate every analytics record with a named individual without an identifier that
allows the provider to locate it.

You can manage local information in the App and manage operating-system backups through your Apple,
Google, or device account.

## Children's privacy

Ratebench is a general-purpose financial reference tool and is not directed to children. We do not
knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal
information, contact us and we will review the request and take appropriate action.

## International processing

Ratebench, its supporting service, and its providers may process information in countries other
than the country where you live. Where required, such processing is handled using safeguards
recognized by applicable data-protection law.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Ratebench, its providers, or applicable law.
The **Last updated** date will be changed, and additional notice will be provided where required.
